## Coordinate system and water categories

- Analysis CRS: UTM zone 42N, `EPSG:32642`.
- 10 m grid: upper-left origin (746580, 3270010), 2,000 × 2,000 pixels.
- 20 m grid: upper-left origin (746580, 3270010), 1,000 × 1,000 pixels.
- Dry window: May–June 2025. Wet window: September 2025.
- NDFI: `(Red − SWIR2) / (Red + SWIR2)` (see source JavaScript for band conversions and SCL masks).
- Candidate-water labels and transition class codes are defined in the notebook itself. **Do not infer class codes from file names alone**; the `NODATA_CODE` for notebook binary products is 225.
